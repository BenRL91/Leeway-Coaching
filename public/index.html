Before your session

A few useful details.

When can I train?

Thursdays only: starts from 5–9 p.m., with all sessions ending by 10 p.m. Eastern. Choose a start time that lets your full session finish before closing. Online times reflect calendar availability; payment and calendar confirmation reserve your session.

Where do sessions take place?

Sessions take place at Centennial Lakes courts
202 Celebration Ln, Acworth, GA 30102
Meet on the court or at the court gate. Directions in Google Maps · Open the court pin in Apple Maps. Court fees are included in the listed price.

Can I come with a partner or a group?

Doubles-team sessions are for exactly two players. Group sessions are for 3–8 players. Include everyone attending, including yourself, in the player list.

Do beginners need equipment?

Pickleball 101 sessions welcome first-time players with no equipment required. Mention that you are new to the game in your request.

How is my session confirmed?

For online coaching bookings, choose an available time and complete secure payment. Your booking is confirmed after payment and calendar scheduling succeed, followed by a calendar invitation. Game breakdowns and email inquiries are arranged directly with Coach Lee; sending an inquiry does not reserve a time.

How do cancellations and weather changes work?

Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours ahead for a full refund or free reschedule. Late cancellations and no-shows are charged in full. If Coach Lee cancels for weather or unsafe courts, choose a refund or reschedule. Read the full policy and email leewaypb@gmail.com to request a change.
