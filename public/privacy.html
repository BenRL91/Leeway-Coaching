Privacy policy

Leeway Pickleball Bookings · Effective October 2, 2026

Coach Lee operates Leeway Pickleball Bookings to arrange coaching sessions in Acworth, Georgia. This policy explains how the booking service handles information.

Information used

Booking information includes the contact’s name and email, player names, coaching goals, session type, date, duration, amount and booking reference. Payment status and Stripe session identifiers are stored to verify payment and track confirmation. Card information is entered through Stripe’s payment form; the booking application does not store full card numbers or security codes.

Google Calendar data

The coach authorizes access to availability and events on calendars the coach owns. The service checks busy times, checks for an existing event for a booking, and creates coaching events containing the players, goals, contact email, session details and payment total. Customers are not asked to authorize access to their own Google accounts.

Google credentials are stored as protected server settings. Calendar data and credentials are used for scheduling and booking administration, not advertising or training generalized AI models. Leeway Pickleball Bookings’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Sharing and service providers

Stripe processes payments. Google Calendar receives event details and the booking contact’s email for calendar invitations. The website and booking records are hosted using OpenAI Sites and its hosting infrastructure. These providers process information to deliver their services. Booking and Google Calendar data are not sold. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

Checkout recovery on your device

Your browser can keep private recovery links for recent checkout attempts for up to 48 hours. These contain random booking references and access secrets, not card details, player names, or email addresses. Anyone using the same browser may be able to open those bookings. Use “Forget saved links on this device” on a shared device. This removes local links, not your booking records. Recovery is limited to the browser where checkout began.

Storage and retention

Booking records are retained as needed to provide coaching, handle booking questions and payment disputes, and meet recordkeeping obligations. Calendar events remain in the coach’s calendar until removed. Access to administrative functions is restricted; hosting providers may process technical request and security information to operate the service.

Your choices

Contact leewaypb@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of booking information. Some records may need to be retained for legal or payment-related obligations. The coach can revoke Google access in Google Account permissions; revoking access stops calendar automation but does not automatically delete existing booking records or events.

Contact and updates

Questions about this policy can be sent to Coach Lee at leewaypb@gmail.com. Changes will be published here with an updated effective date.

Game breakdown footage and tracking
