Refunds, booking issues & policies
Receipts and booking issues
In your live Stripe account, open Customer emails and enable Successful payments and Refunds. Test receipt emails must be sent manually from a sandbox payment’s receipt history.
For a refund: cancel the booking here to release its time, open the matching payment in Stripe, issue the appropriate refund, then use Check Stripe refund status here. Cancelling alone never refunds a payment.
For a paid booking awaiting confirmation: do not ask the customer to pay again. Check Google Calendar for conflicts, then use Retry confirmation. If the time cannot be honored, agree a new time or cancel and refund in Stripe.
The booking list flags issues when opened or refreshed. Background email alerts are not configured.
Booking policy
Approved. Displayed in the booking flow and terms.
  • Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before the session for a full refund or a free reschedule.
  • Cancellations within 24 hours and no-shows are charged the full session price. Coach Lee may make exceptions for emergencies.
  • If Coach Lee cancels because of weather, unsafe courts, or another coaching-side issue, choose a full refund or free reschedule.
  • If weather interrupts a session, receive a refund or credit for the unused coaching time.
  • Late arrivals finish at the original end time.
  • For duo and group bookings, the booking contact handles changes for the group. Player-count changes at least 24 hours ahead are repriced at the published rate for the revised group size. Later reductions do not reduce the booked price.
  • Request changes by emailing leewaypb@gmail.com.
