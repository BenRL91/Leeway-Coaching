About the service

This booking service is operated by Coach Lee for Leeway Pickleball coaching in Acworth, Georgia. Public online booking is not yet open. Access during setup is limited to authorized testing.

Booking and payment

Provide accurate contact and player information and only submit details you are authorized to share. Review the displayed session, location, duration and total before paying. A requested time or temporary checkout hold is not a confirmed booking. Confirmation requires verified payment and successful calendar scheduling.

Stripe processes payments. Sandbox transactions are tests and do not purchase a coaching session. If payment is received but scheduling remains pending, contact Coach Lee with your booking reference rather than paying again.

Changes, cancellations and weather

  • Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before the session for a full refund or a free reschedule.
  • Cancellations within 24 hours and no-shows are charged the full session price. Coach Lee may make exceptions for emergencies.
  • If Coach Lee cancels because of weather, unsafe courts, or another coaching-side issue, choose a full refund or free reschedule.
  • If weather interrupts a session, receive a refund or credit for the unused coaching time.
  • Late arrivals finish at the original end time.
  • For duo and group bookings, the booking contact handles changes for the group. Player-count changes at least 24 hours ahead are repriced at the published rate for the revised group size. Later reductions do not reduce the booked price.
  • Request changes by emailing leewaypb@gmail.com.

Changes and refunds are handled directly with Coach Lee; they are not automatically processed by this website. This policy applies to on-court coaching sessions. Game breakdown delivery and any separate terms are agreed before purchase.

Responsible use

Do not submit fraudulent bookings, interfere with the service or attempt to access another person’s booking information. Keep your booking confirmation link private.

Availability and privacy

Scheduling depends on court availability and the operation of payment and calendar providers. If a technical problem affects a booking, contact Coach Lee for assistance. Personal information is handled as described in our Privacy policy.
