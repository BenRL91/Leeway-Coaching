Leeway Pickleball Bookings

Scheduling and payment for pickleball coaching with Coach Lee.

This service coordinates individual, doubles and group coaching at Centennial Lakes courts in Acworth, Georgia. Online booking is currently being prepared for public launch.

How booking works

The booking service checks the coach’s availability, reserves a selected time during checkout, and uses Stripe to process payment. Once payment is verified, it creates a coaching event in the coach’s Google Calendar and requests an invitation to the booking contact’s email address.

Google Calendar connection

Only the coach connects a Google account. Calendar access is used to check availability and manage coaching events on the coach’s calendar. Customers do not need to connect their Google accounts or grant access to their personal calendars.
